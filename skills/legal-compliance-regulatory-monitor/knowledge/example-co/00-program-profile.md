| Coverage amounts | $10,000–$125,000 online; higher amounts available by phone |

**Critical compliance flags:**
- Premium escalation every 5 years MUST be disclosed clearly in all advertising
- Coverage ceiling ($125K standard online) must not be obscured

### Guaranteed Issue Final Expense Insurance
| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Eligible members (most states) | Ages 55–85 |
| Eligible spouses (most states) | Ages 50–85 |
| Eligible in Washington | Members and spouses ages 55–75 only |
| Coverage amounts | Up to $25,000; higher amounts by phone |
| Medical exam required | None — **no health questions** |
| Acceptance | Guaranteed — cannot be declined for health reasons |
| **Waiting period (CRITICAL)** | **3-year graded benefit**: if insured dies of natural causes within first 3 years, beneficiary receives 120% of premiums paid — NOT full death benefit |

**Critical compliance flags — HIGHEST RISK PRODUCT:**
- The 3-year graded benefit waiting period MUST be prominently disclosed in all advertising
- "Guaranteed issue" cannot be emphasized without equivalent emphasis on the waiting period limitation
- Prohibited: claims that imply full death benefit is available from day one
- MT exclusion must appear in all materials sold or displayed in MT
- WA age band restriction must appear in WA-targeted materials

## 4. Dual-Brand Compliance Framework

This program operates under two independent compliance standards. Materials must satisfy BOTH before publication.

### Layer 1 — Example Life Insurance Compliance Standards
- Example Life marketing compliance review
- Home-state insurance department advertising rules, plus NYDFS rules for business sold in New York
- NAIC Life Insurance Advertising Model Regulation (#582) as adopted by each state
- NAIC Unfair Trade Practices Act (#880) standards
- All-state filing and approval requirements per each state's adopted advertising rules

### Layer 2 — Partner Org Brand & Licensing Standards
- Partner Org brand usage guidelines (logo, name, "Partner Org-branded" descriptor rules)
- Partner Org brand standards (positive, capable imagery of older adults; no fear or decline framing)
- Partner Org member trust positioning — materials may not misrepresent Partner Org's role as a neutral organization
- Endorsement risk: under the FTC Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255), any claim that Partner Org-branded coverage is "best" or "recommended" for older adults, without disclosing Partner Org's licensing-fee interest, could be treated as a deceptive endorsement and invite regulatory or private legal challenge

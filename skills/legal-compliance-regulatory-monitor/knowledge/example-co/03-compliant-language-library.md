# Example Life Insurance Program — Compliant Language Library

**Program:** Example Life Insurance Program (Example Life/Partner Org)  
**Last Updated:** 2025  
**Purpose:** Provides pre-reviewed language patterns for common claims, benefit descriptions, and disclosures. Use as a starting point — all materials still require compliance review before publication. Do not use language from this library without confirming it matches current policy form terms.

---

## How to Use This Library

- **Green (Approved Pattern):** Language that aligns with regulatory requirements and passes standard review. Use as template.
- **Yellow (Caution — Context-Dependent):** Language that may be compliant depending on context, placement, and adjacent disclosures. Flag for review.
- **Red (Prohibited Pattern):** Language known to create compliance risk. Do not use.

---

## 1. Program Identity & Partner Org Relationship

### Green — Approved Patterns

| Context | Approved Language |
|---|---|
| Program name (first reference) | "The Example Life Insurance Program, issued by Example Life Insurance Company" |
| Program name (subsequent reference) | "Example Life Insurance" or "the Program" |
| Relationship descriptor | "Example Life Insurance Company underwrites life insurance for Partner Org members through the Example Life Insurance Program." |
| Partner Org role | "Partner Org makes this program available to its members." |
| Licensing-fee disclosure (where required) | "Partner Org receives a licensing fee from Example Life for use of the Partner Org name and logo. These fees help fund Partner Org's member programs." |
| Partner Org description | "Partner Org is a membership association offering financial-wellness and active-living resources to adults 45 and older." |

### Red — Prohibited Patterns

| Prohibited Language | Reason |
|---|---|
| "Partner Org-endorsed coverage" | Implies independent endorsement; obscures licensing/licensing-fee relationship |
| "Recommended by Partner Org" | Same as above |
| "Partner Org's best life insurance option for seniors" | Superlative endorsement claim; undisclosed material connection under FTC Endorsement Guides |
| "Partner Org has chosen Example Life as its exclusive provider" | Exclusivity claim requires legal verification; "chosen" implies independent evaluation |
| "Backed by Partner Org" | Implies financial guarantee from Partner Org; Partner Org is not the underwriter |

## 2. Eligibility Claims

### Green — Approved Patterns

| Context | Approved Language |
|---|---|
| Primary eligibility | "Available to Partner Org members age 55 and older and their eligible spouses." |
| Spouse eligibility (Term Life) | "Eligible spouses ages 50 to 70 may also apply." |
| Spouse eligibility (Whole Life) | "Eligible spouses ages 50 to 80 may also apply." |
| Spouse eligibility (Guaranteed Issue) | "Eligible spouses ages 50 to 85 may also apply." [Note: add WA restriction for WA-targeted materials] |
| Membership requirement | "Partner Org membership is required. Membership is open to adults age 45 and older." |
| Anyone can join | "Joining is simple — Partner Org membership is open to any adult age 45 or older." |

### Yellow — Caution Patterns

| Language | Condition for Use |
|---|---|
| "For people 55 and older" | Only permissible if Partner Org membership requirement is disclosed in the same placement |
| "Designed for Partner Org members" | Fine as a framing statement; must not imply eligibility is automatic without membership |

### Red — Prohibited Patterns

| Prohibited Language | Reason |
|---|---|
| "Available to adults age 55 and older" (without membership caveat) | Hides Partner Org membership requirement |
| "Anyone can get this coverage" | False — membership required |

## 3. No Medical Exam Claims

### Green — Approved Patterns

| Context | Approved Language |
|---|---|
| General | "No medical exam required." |
| Term Life and Whole Life | "Answer a few health questions — no medical exam needed." |
| Guaranteed Issue | "No health questions required. Acceptance is guaranteed." [Must be paired with waiting period disclosure — see Section 6] |

### Yellow — Caution Patterns

| Language | Condition for Use |
|---|---|
| "Easy to qualify" | Only for Guaranteed Issue with waiting period disclosure present |
| "No doctor visits required" | Accurate but should be positioned carefully to not overstate ease of coverage onset |

### Red — Prohibited Patterns

| Prohibited Language | Reason |
|---|---|
| "No questions asked" (for Term or Whole Life) | These products DO ask health questions; only Guaranteed Issue has no health questions |
| "Automatic approval" | No product has automatic approval without at least a membership verification step |

## 4. Benefit Claims — Term Life Insurance

### Green — Approved Patterns

| Context | Approved Language |
|---|---|
| Product description | "Step-Rate Term Life Insurance provides coverage up to age 85 with a death benefit that remains level throughout the term." |
| Premium structure | "Your premiums will increase when you enter each new 5-year age band." |
| Initial rate framing | "Your initial monthly rate is $[X], with rates increasing as you age." |
| Coverage range | "Choose coverage from $10,000 up to $125,000. Higher coverage amounts may be available by calling [number]." |
| Conversion right | "You can convert your term policy to permanent life insurance before age 80." |
| AutoPay discount | "Choose AutoPay and save up to $15 per year on your premiums." |

### Red — Prohibited Patterns

| Prohibited Language | Reason |
|---|---|
| "Lock in your rate" (without disclosure) | Rates are NOT locked in — they increase every 5 years |
| "Level premiums" for Term Life | Term premiums are NOT level — this is the description for Whole Life |
| "Lifetime coverage" for Term Life | Term expires at age 85; it is not lifetime coverage |
| "Protect your family for life" (for Term, without clarification) | Implies permanent coverage; must clarify "until age 85" |

## 5. Benefit Claims — Whole Life Insurance

### Green — Approved Patterns

| Context | Approved Language |
|---|---|
| Product description | "Whole Life Insurance provides permanent, lifelong coverage with premiums that never increase." |
| Level premium | "Your monthly premium stays the same — it never goes up." |
| Coverage range | "Choose coverage from $[X] up to $75,000 online. Higher amounts may be available by calling [number]." |
| Paid-up status | "Once your policy is paid up, typically around age 100, you no longer need to make premium payments — and your coverage continues." |
| Permanence | "Your coverage lasts your entire lifetime — it never expires." |

### Yellow — Caution Patterns

| Language | Condition for Use |
|---|---|
| "Build a legacy" | Fine as positioning language; must not imply a savings or investment component |
| "Leave something behind for your family" | Permissible; ensure coverage amount context is present |

### Red — Prohibited Patterns

| Prohibited Language | Reason |
|---|---|
| "Whole life insurance grows in value" (without cash value qualification) | This product description must be verified against the policy form; do not imply investment growth unless the product has a cash value feature |
| "Your money back if you cancel" | Not accurate unless surrender value language is in the policy form — verify before using |

## 6. Benefit Claims — Guaranteed Issue Final Expense

### Green — Approved Patterns

| Context | Approved Language |
|---|---|
| Product description | "Guaranteed Issue Final Expense Insurance is available to Partner Org members ages 55 to 85. No health questions are required — your acceptance is guaranteed." |
| Waiting period disclosure — SHORT form | "This policy has a waiting period. Benefits paid in the first 3 years for death by natural causes will equal 120% of premiums paid." |
| Waiting period disclosure — LONG form | "Please note: This policy includes a graded benefit period. If the insured dies of natural causes during the first 3 years of the policy, the benefit paid to your beneficiaries will equal 120% of all premiums you have paid, not the full death benefit amount. Full benefits are payable for death due to an accident at any time." |
| MT disclaimer | "Not available in Montana." |
| WA age band (WA-targeted materials only) | "Available to Partner Org members and their eligible spouses ages 55 to 75 in Washington." |
| Coverage range | "Choose coverage from $[X] up to $25,000. Higher amounts may be available by calling [number]." |
| Accidental death benefit | "If death occurs as a result of an accident, the full benefit is payable from day one." |

### Red — Prohibited Patterns

| Prohibited Language | Reason |
|---|---|
| "Full coverage from day one" | False — graded benefit applies in first 3 years for natural causes |
| "No waiting period" | False — 3-year waiting period exists |
| "Your family gets $[X] immediately" | Implies immediate full payout; waiting period may apply |
| "Guaranteed payout of $[X]" (without waiting period qualification) | Misleading — waiting period creates condition on payout |
| "Available in all states" | False — not available in MT |

## 7. Benefit Claims — Accelerated Benefit Rider (Accelerated Death Benefit)

### Green — Approved Patterns

| Context | Approved Language |
|---|---|
| Feature description | "This policy includes an Accelerated Benefit Rider. If you are diagnosed with a qualifying illness, you may be able to access up to 40% of your policy's death benefit while you are still living." |
| Qualifying illness trigger | "The Accelerated Benefit Rider is available if you are diagnosed with a qualifying terminal, chronic, or critical illness, as defined in your policy." |
| State availability disclaimer | "Accelerated Benefit Rider is not available in all states." |
| Benefit limit | "You may access up to 40% of your policy's face amount as a accelerated benefit." |

### Red — Prohibited Patterns

| Prohibited Language | Reason |
|---|---|
| "Access your benefit anytime" | False — qualifying illness required |
| "Use your life insurance while you're alive" (standalone, without qualifier) | Must be paired with qualifying illness requirement |
| "Cash out your policy" | Incorrect framing; this is not a cash value or surrender |
| "Accelerated benefits available to all policyholders" | Not available in all states |

## 8. Urgency and Enrollment Window Claims

### Green — Approved Patterns

| Context | Approved Language |
|---|---|
| Genuine enrollment deadline | "This special enrollment period ends [date]." [Only if a genuine, verifiable deadline exists] |
| Age-based urgency (factual) | "The younger you enroll, the lower your initial rate." |
| Open enrollment (when applicable) | "Enroll now — no medical exam required." |

### Yellow — Caution Patterns

| Language | Condition for Use |
|---|---|
| "Act before rates change" | Only if a rate change is genuinely scheduled; must be verified with actuarial/product team before use |
| "Limited time offer" | Only if an actual time limit exists; must include end date |

### Red — Prohibited Patterns

| Prohibited Language | Reason |
|---|---|
| "You may lose your eligibility soon" (without factual basis) | Manufactured urgency; exploits health anxiety |
| "This offer may not be available after [arbitrary date]" | False urgency unless tied to genuine business rule |
| "Act now before it's too late" | Fear-based urgency; violates Partner Org brand standards and FTC senior marketing guidance |
| "Your age is working against you — don't wait" | Exploits aging anxiety; prohibited by Partner Org brand standards |
| Countdown timers (web/email) without genuine expiration | Creates false urgency |

## 9. Financial Strength Claims

### Green — Approved Patterns

| Context | Approved Language |
|---|---|
| AM Best citation | "Example Life Insurance Company is rated [rating] ([category]) for financial strength by A.M. Best, as of [date]. This rating is subject to change." |
| Abbreviated reference | "Rated [rating] by A.M. Best (as of [date])." |
| Third-party satisfaction rating | "Example Life ranked #[N] of [total] companies in [study name, year] for customer satisfaction." |

### Red — Prohibited Patterns

| Prohibited Language | Reason |
|---|---|
| "#1 in customer satisfaction" | Not supported by the current third-party ranking — cite the actual rank only |
| "Best-rated life insurance company" (unqualified) | Superlative must be substantiated by a specific, cited source |
| AM Best rating without source and date | Required elements for substantiated financial strength claim |

## 10. Standard Disclosure Blocks

### Short Form (Space-Constrained Placements — Digital Ads, Social)
> *"The Example Life Insurance Program, issued by Example Life Insurance Company, 123 Main Street, Anytown, ST 00000. Not available in all states. Premiums increase with age. Guaranteed Issue policies have a waiting period. Partner Org membership required."*

### Long Form (Direct Mail, Landing Pages)
> *"The Example Life Insurance Program is issued by Example Life Insurance Company, 123 Main Street, Anytown, ST 00000. Partner Org receives a licensing fee from Example Life for use of its name and logo. Partner Org membership is required to purchase. Not available in all states or for all ages. Step-Rate Term Life Insurance premiums increase when you enter a new 5-year age band. Guaranteed Issue Final Expense Insurance has a waiting period: if the insured dies of natural causes in the first 3 policy years, the benefit paid to your beneficiaries is 120% of premiums paid. Guaranteed Issue Final Expense is not available in Montana. In Washington, Guaranteed Issue Final Expense is available to members and eligible spouses ages 55–75. Accelerated Benefit Rider is not available in all states. This is a solicitation for insurance."*

### Replacement Notice (Where Replacement Disclosure is Required by State)
> *"If you are replacing existing life insurance coverage, you have the right to review your current policy before deciding to replace it. If you decide to replace your existing policy, you should terminate it only after the new policy has been issued and you have decided to accept it."*

# Case Studies

How successful products implement the Hook — plus instructive failures. Each maps Trigger · Action · Variable Reward · Investment.

## Instagram — visual Tribe hook
- **Trigger** — "X liked your photo", badges (external); boredom, loneliness, FOMO, validation (internal).
- **Action** — open → scroll; one tap, immediate content.
- **Reward** — Tribe (likes/comments/followers, unpredictable), Hunt (feed, disappearing Stories), Self (posting an aesthetic grid).
- **Investment** — follow (loads feed), post (loads notifications), build followers, curate grid.
- **Why** — multiple reward types; every post loads a trigger; primal internal trigger; frictionless infinite scroll; likes trickle in over time.

## Slack — workplace communication hook
- **Trigger** — notifications, @mentions, badges (external); FOMO on work talk, staying informed (internal).
- **Action** — scan and reply; always open.
- **Reward** — Tribe (recognition, humor), Hunt (info/decisions), Self (being "in the know").
- **Investment** — join channels (more triggers), customize, add integrations, build searchable history.
- **Why** — work adds obligation on top of internal triggers; variable timing of important messages; investment compounds; social switching cost. (Note the manufactured urgency via presence indicators and real-time expectation — an ethics caution.)

## Duolingo — gamified learning hook
- **Trigger** — daily reminders, streak warnings, leaderboards (external); guilt over a broken streak, self-improvement (internal).
- **Action** — a 5–10 min lesson; bite-sized, game-like.
- **Reward** — Self (XP, streaks, level/skill progress), Tribe (leaderboard), Hunt (bonus events).
- **Investment** — streak (high switching cost), XP/levels, skill trees, friends.
- **Why** — streak = loss aversion + sunk cost + identity ("I'm someone who does Duolingo daily"); immediate rewards; low time commitment; visible progress. Ethical because the user's goal (learn) aligns with the engagement (daily practice).

## Pinterest — visual Hunt hook
- **Trigger** — email digests, "new ideas for you" (external); seeking inspiration/planning, aspiration (internal).
- **Action** — browse and pin; visual scanning, minimal effort.
- **Reward** — Hunt (discovery), Self (curating boards), Tribe (repins, secondary).
- **Investment** — boards, pins, followed topics, saved ideas.
- **Why** — low-effort visual discovery; aspiration ("future me") is internally motivating; boards become personal archives; infinite content. Unusual: the reward is often *planning for future action*, so repeat engagement happens without "completion."

## Asana — B2B SaaS hook
- **Trigger** — assignments, due-date reminders, mentions (external); overwhelm, fear of dropping balls (internal).
- **Action** — update/create tasks; quick.
- **Reward** — Self (completion, control), Tribe (team visibility), Hunt (cross-project status).
- **Investment** — projects, workflows, team habits, history.
- **B2B specifics** — habit must form at the *team* level; work obligation strengthens external triggers; switching costs are massive (history + training); multiple stakeholders in the investment.

## Instructive Failures
- **Google+** — no internal trigger (nobody needed another network), forced/intrusive external triggers, weak rewards from a thin audience, cold-start made investment feel pointless. *You can't force habits.*
- **Quibi** — misread the circumstance ("in-between moments" already owned by TikTok/YouTube), action too complex (subscription + app + commitment), scripted = predictable rewards, watching didn't load next triggers. *Understand what users already "hire" for the job.*
- **Vine** — strong community and format, but creator investment went unrewarded (no monetization) while rivals paid creators, and the platform didn't load next triggers. *Investment must benefit the user, not just the platform.*

## Cross-Cutting Patterns
- **Multiple reward types** — ≥2 of Tribe/Hunt/Self, primary aligned with the core job.
- **Investment loads triggers** — post→engagement notice, join channel→its messages, build streak→its reminder, pin→recommendations.
- **Internal-trigger alignment** — each solves a real emotion (loneliness, FOMO, guilt, aspiration).
- **Frictionless first action** — scroll, scan, tap.

Warning signs in your product: users need constant reminders (no internal trigger), engagement drops after novelty (weak/predictable rewards), usage doesn't grow over time (no investment loop), high acquisition/low retention, users call it "useful but forgettable" (functional, no emotional hook).

# Product Applications

The Hook adapts to product type. For each, the four phases plus what's distinctive. Also the home for **Action design** — the simplest behavior in anticipation of reward, governed by Fogg's B = Motivation × Ability × Trigger (reduce friction before boosting motivation; every extra step/field/choice loses users).

## Adaptation Framework

For any product: (1) identify the job — what progress the user seeks; (2) find the internal trigger — the emotion before use; (3) minimize action friction; (4) align rewards with real value; (5) make investment meaningful; (6) check ethics — does habitual use serve the user?

## By Product Type

**B2B SaaS** — users may be *required* to use it; habits form at the *team* level; value is productivity. Trigger: assignments, mentions, due dates (external); FOMO on work, staying organized (internal). Action: fit existing workflow, completable in seconds. Reward: Self (progress), Tribe (recognition), Hunt (find info). Investment: data, integrations, team adoption, learned skill. *(Slack, Notion, Salesforce, Asana.)*

**E-commerce** — not daily use; the goal is being first choice at purchase intent. Trigger: email/retargeting (external); "I wonder if…" curiosity (internal). Action: browse, add to cart/wishlist. Reward: Hunt (deals/discovery), Self (smart purchase). Investment: saved prefs, order history, loyalty tiers, reviews. *(Amazon one-click; Pinterest → commerce.)*

**Health & fitness** — motivation fluctuates, results are delayed, ethical stakes are high. Trigger: reminders, streaks (external); guilt, aspiration (internal). Action: log/complete. Reward: Self (progress, PRs), Tribe (accountability), Hunt (what works for you). Investment: history, streaks, community. **Build in rest days; discourage over-exercise.** *(Peloton, MyFitnessPal.)*

**Education** — make difficult things feel achievable; balance engagement with real learning. Trigger: reminders, streak warnings (external); curiosity, career anxiety (internal). Action: a *short* lesson. Reward: Self (mastery), Hunt (new knowledge), Tribe (compare progress). Investment: progress, certifications, sunk time. *(Duolingo, Masterclass, Coursera.)*

**Productivity** — reduce cognitive load, don't add it. Trigger: due dates, reviews (external); overwhelm, need for control (internal). Action: quick capture. Reward: Self (control, completion), Hunt (optimizing the system). Investment: accumulated tasks/notes, custom workflows. *(Todoist, Obsidian.)*

**Social & community** — network effects, user-generated (inherently variable) content, powerful and risky social dynamics. Trigger: notifications, tags (external); loneliness, boredom, FOMO (internal). Action: scroll/post/engage. Reward: Tribe (validation, belonging), Hunt (discovery), Self (creation). Investment: network, content, reputation. **Provide usage controls; don't optimize purely for time spent.** *(Instagram, Discord.)*

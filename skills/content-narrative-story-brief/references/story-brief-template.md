# Story Brief Template

Fill-in structure for Step 6. Output as a single markdown document.

```markdown
# Story Brief: [Title]

## Driver / Why Now
[One to three sentences: why this story exists, why now]

## Outcomes
1. [Outcome] — Ask | Inform/Appreciate
2. [Outcome] — Ask | Inform/Appreciate
3. [Outcome] — Ask | Inform/Appreciate
(1-3 total)

## Audience
[Board | Exec | Peer | Custom]
[If custom: role, current belief, what they weigh, likely objection, framing depth]
**Concrete specifics:** [known concern, relevant history, likely individual reaction, or recent context shaping how this lands]

## Framing (depth per audience playbook)
**Why:** [driver, restated for this audience]
**Ask(s):** [what's being asked, if any]
**Takeaway(s):** [what should be understood or remembered]

## Story Arc: [Model Name]

### [Beat 1 name]
Core message: [one sentence]
Evidence: [supporting points]

### [Beat 2 name]
Core message: [one sentence]
Evidence: [supporting points]

### [Beat 3 name, etc.]
Core message: [one sentence]
Evidence: [supporting points]

## Stress-Test Notes
- So-what result per beat: [pass/fail + note for any beat that needed revision]
- Logline: "Because [driver], we're asking [audience] to [ask/takeaway], which matters because [why]."

## Supporting-Detail Appendix (optional)
[Backing material, data, or documentation for anyone who wants to go deeper. Not part of the primary read.]
```

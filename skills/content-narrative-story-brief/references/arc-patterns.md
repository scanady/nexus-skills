# Arc Patterns

Four narrative models to offer at Step 3. Present all that plausibly fit (2-4 of them), each with the one-line fit description below, and let the user pick. Do not pick on their behalf.

## Situation-Complication-Resolution (SCR)

**Fit:** Status-quo-disrupting stories, where the audience needs to first agree something has changed before they'll accept what comes next.

**Beats:**
1. Situation — the stable state the audience already recognizes
2. Complication — what changed or was discovered that breaks that stability
3. Resolution — what the audience is being asked to decide or do about it

## Pyramid Principle

**Fit:** Answer-first efficiency. Best for busy or senior audiences who want the conclusion before the reasoning, and for Inform/Appreciate outcomes where there's no persuasion to build toward.

**Beats:**
1. Answer — the conclusion or recommendation, stated directly
2. Key line — the 2-4 supporting arguments, each one sentence
3. Support — evidence and detail under each supporting argument, ordered by strength

## Problem-Agitate-Solve (PAS)

**Fit:** Persuasive stories with an Ask outcome, where the audience doesn't yet feel the urgency of the problem.

**Beats:**
1. Problem — the issue stated plainly
2. Agitate — the cost of leaving it unresolved, made concrete
3. Solve — the proposed path and why it resolves the problem

## Before-After-Bridge

**Fit:** Transformation narratives, change initiatives, or anything where the driver is a shift already underway.

**Beats:**
1. Before — the current state
2. After — the target state once the change lands
3. Bridge — what closes the gap between them, and what's being asked of the audience to get there

## Selecting Among Them

When more than one fits, narrow using the outcomes from Step 1:
- Any Ask outcome present → PAS or SCR usually outperform Pyramid Principle, since they build the case rather than lead with it
- Only Inform/Appreciate outcomes → Pyramid Principle usually fits best
- Driver is a change or transformation → Before-After-Bridge is worth including in the options even alongside others

Still present the full shortlist and let the user choose; this narrowing only decides which 2-4 to surface.

# Benchmarks

Reference points for B2B SaaS. Vendors, stage, and ACV shift them. Use as sanity range, not as target. Replace with own history when you have it ([calibration-method.md](calibration-method.md)).

## Revenue retention

| Level | NRR | GRR |
|---|---|---|
| Best in class | over 130% | over 95% |
| Excellent | 120-130% | 92-95% |
| Good | 110-120% | 90-92% |
| Acceptable | 100-110% | 85-90% |
| Below target | 90-100% | 80-85% |
| Concerning | under 90% | under 80% |

Healthy growth needs NRR over 110% and GRR over 90%.

| Segment | NRR median / top / bottom | GRR median / top / bottom |
|---|---|---|
| Enterprise (over $100K ARR) | 115 / 130+ / 105 | 95 / 98 / 90 |
| Mid-market ($25K-$100K) | 108 / 120 / 98 | 90 / 95 / 85 |
| SMB (under $25K) | 95 / 105 / 85 | 82 / 90 / 75 |

NRR high, GRR low: churn replaced by survivors' growth. Unsustainable. GRR high, NRR low: sticky but no expansion. Both high: compounding.

## Health portfolio shape

| Band | Target share | Alert |
|---|---|---|
| Green | 60-70% | under 50%: portfolio review |
| Yellow | 20-30% | over 35%: systemic issue |
| Red | 5-10% | over 15%: executive intervention |

Average score target: enterprise over 78 (median 72, top quartile 82); mid-market over 75 (68, 78); SMB over 70 (65, 75).
Dimension medians (enterprise / mid-market / SMB): usage 72 / 68 / 60; engagement 70 / 62 / 55; support 78 / 72 / 65; relationship 68 / 60 / 50.

## Metric links

Health down: churn up. NPS up: NRR up. First value faster: GRR up. Adoption up: expansion up. Escalations up: NPS down. More contacts per account: GRR up.

# Plain English Alternatives

Use in Sweep 1 (Clarity) and Sweep 5 (Specificity). Find pompous word, swap for plain one. Pick the alternative that fit the sentence. Never swap if the plain word change meaning.

Basis: Plain English Campaign A-Z of Alternative Words, Australian Government Style Manual (2024), plainlanguage.gov. Regrouped here by what the edit is doing.

---

## 1. Delete the Phrase

Add nothing. Cut, then reread. Sentence still work = cut was right.

absolutely · actually · a total of · all things being equal · as a matter of fact · at the end of the day · at this moment in time · basically · in due course (say when, or "soon") · in the final analysis · it should be understood · last but not least · obviously · of course · quite · really · the fact of the matter is · to all intents and purposes · very

"I am of the opinion that" → "I think". "Currently" → "now", or nothing.

## 6. "Must" Phrasing

"It is compulsory / mandatory / obligatory" → "you must". Address the reader directly.

## Marketing Caveat

Some marketing buzzwords sit on this skill's Banned List (`writing_styles.md`). Common swaps: "leverage" → "use", "seamless" → "smooth", "robust" → "strong", "innovative" → "new", "cutting-edge" → "new". When the swap still read generic ("smooth", "strong"), skip the synonym. Use the Sweep 5 move: replace with a number or example.

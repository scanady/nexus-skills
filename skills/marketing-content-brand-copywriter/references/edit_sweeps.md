# Seven Sweeps: Edit Pass for Marketing Copy

One pass = one question. Fix all at once = miss half. Run sweeps in order. Each sweep read whole draft with only that question in head.

Edit = enhance, not rewrite. Keep core message. Keep author voice. Every change get one-line reason.

---

## Before Sweep 1

Know three things. Missing one = ask, or apply Defaults in SKILL.md and say so.

1. Goal: awareness, conversion, or retention?
2. Reader action: what exact click or reply?
3. Audience: one person, described.

Read draft once. No edits yet.

---

## Run Order and Regression Rule

Order: Clarity → Voice → So What → Prove It → Specificity → Emotion → Zero Risk.

Late sweeps add words. Added words break early sweeps. After each sweep, **regression check**: reread only changed lines against exit tests of earlier sweeps. Break found = fix now, then continue. Skip full re-read. Do full re-read once, after Sweep 7.

---

## Sweep 1: Clarity

**Question:** Reader get it on first read?

**Hunt:**
- Sentence over 25 words, or two ideas in one
- Pronoun with two possible targets
- Jargon, insider term, unexplained acronym
- Point buried under qualifiers
- Missing context reader lacks

**Fix moves:**
- Split. One idea per sentence.
- Replace pronoun with noun.
- Move point to front. Cut hedge.
- Swap abstract noun for concrete one.
- Pompous word → plain word. See `plain_english_alternatives.md`.

**Mechanical check (no tool needed):** count passive verbs, filler words (very, really, just, actually, basically), "in order to", stacked nouns. Every hit = candidate line.

**Exit test:** Read each sentence cold. Zero re-reads needed. One main idea per section. Copy speak to "you".

---

## Sweep 2: Voice and Tone

**Question:** Same speaker whole way?

**Hunt:**
- Casual start, corporate finish (or reverse)
- "We" in one paragraph, "the company" in next
- Joke in one spot, solemn in next, unplanned
- Random technical term in plain copy
- Any hit from Banned List in `writing_styles.md`

**Fix moves:**
- Pick the voice from `FOUNDER_CONTEXT.md`, or from the draft's strongest paragraph. Rewrite outliers to match.
- Pick one pronoun for the brand. Hold it.
- Read aloud in head. Stumble = mark.

**Exit test:** Cover the byline. Whole piece still sound like one person. Zero banned phrases. Contractions used.

Regression: Clarity.

---

## Sweep 3: So What

**Question:** Each claim answer "why should I care?"

**Test:** Say "so what?" after every sentence. No deeper benefit = broken.

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Our platform uses AI-powered analytics | Our analytics flag the trends you'd miss by hand, so you decide in half the time |

**Hunt:**
- Feature list with no outcome
- Impressive claim that change nothing for reader
- Company achievement reader gain nothing from
- Missing "which means..." bridge

**Fix moves:**
- Add "which means" after feature. Write the outcome. Delete the "which means" words if sentence read fine without.
- Cut claim that no bridge can save.
- Features outnumber benefits 2:1 = big problem. Cut features to the 3–5 reader value most.

**Exit test:** Every feature tie to a thing reader want. No empty brag left.

Regression: Voice, Clarity.

---

## Sweep 4: Prove It

**Question:** Each claim have evidence within eye distance?

**Hunt:** "trusted by thousands" (which?), "industry-leading" (by whose measure?), "customers love us" (show quote), "best", "#1", "fastest", result claim with no number.

**Proof types:** named testimonial with detail, case study, stat with source, third-party review or award, customer logo, review score, guarantee.

**Fix moves:**
- Proof exists nearby → move it next to claim.
- Proof exists nowhere → soften claim, or cut it, or flag to author.
- **Never invent proof.** No made-up name, number, quote, or logo. Missing proof = write `[NEED: source for "…"]` and tell author.

**Exit test:** Zero unearned superlatives. Every number have source or author confirmation.

Regression: So What, Voice, Clarity.

---

## Sweep 5: Specificity

**Question:** Could a rival print this line unchanged?

**Hunt:** improve, enhance, optimize, empower; adjectives doing noun work; round numbers that smell made up; outcome with no timeframe; claim with no example.

| Vague | Specific |
|---|---|
| Save time | Save 4 hours every week |
| Many customers | 2,847 teams |
| Fast results | Results in 14 days |
| Great support | Reply within 2 hours |

**Fix moves:** add number, timeframe, name, or example. Use author's real figures only. Cannot make line specific = line is filler. Cut it.

**Exit test:** Line could not describe a different product.

Regression: Prove It, So What, Voice, Clarity.

---

## Sweep 6: Heightened Emotion

**Question:** Reader feel anything?

**Dimensions:** pain of today, anger at failed alternatives, fear of missing out, wish for change, pride in smart pick, relief.

**Hunt:** flat informational block where reader should lean in; pain named but not felt; goal stated but not pictured.

**Fix moves:**
- Show the "before" in one concrete scene (Ogilvy: detail, not adjective).
- Add sensory detail.
- Micro-story, two or three sentences.
- Shared moment reader recognize.
- One question that make reader pause.

**Guardrails:** emotion must be true to product. No fake vulnerability, no manufactured fear, no stacked fragments for punch. Feeling serve message. If it only manipulate, cut.

**Exit test:** Pick the flattest section. Reader still feel something there. Nothing feel staged.

Regression: Specificity, Prove It, So What, Voice, Clarity.

---

## Sweep 7: Zero Risk

**Question:** Anything left that make reader hesitate at the CTA?

**Hunt (near CTA first):**
- Ask for commitment before trust earned
- Objection raised, never answered
- Fine print that create doubt
- Hidden cost or surprise
- Vague next step ("Contact us")
- No trust signal near button

**Risk reducers:** money-back guarantee, free trial, "no credit card", "cancel anytime", proof beside CTA, plain statement of what happen after click, privacy line.

**Fix moves:**
- List every reason to hesitate. Answer each one in copy, or add reducer.
- CTA name the result: "Get the free template", not "Submit".
- Use only terms the business really offer. No fake scarcity. No invented guarantee. Unknown = ask.

**Exit test:** Reader know exact next step, cost, and what they risk. Single CTA.

Regression: full re-read, all seven exit tests, once.

---

## Quick Pass (when time short)

Run Sweeps 1, 3, 5 only, plus word-level cut list:

- Cut: very, really, extremely, incredibly, just, actually, basically, "in order to" → "to", things, stuff.
- Passive → active. "Make a decision" → "decide".
- Max one adverb per paragraph.
- Paragraph = one topic, 2–4 sentences for web.
- Front-load the important part.

Label output "quick pass" so author know sweeps 2, 4, 6, 7 skipped.

## Frequent Problems → First Fix

| Symptom | Sweep | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Wall of features | 3 | "which means" after each |
| Corporate speak | 1, 2 | Ask "how would a person say this?" |
| Opens with company history | 3 | Open with reader problem or outcome |
| CTA buried or vague | 7 | Make it early, obvious, repeated, named |
| "Customers love us", no evidence | 4 | Quote, number, case reference |
| "We help businesses grow" | 5 | Who, how, how much |
| Speaks to everyone | 1 | Pick one reader |
| Every capability listed | 3 | Keep top 3–5 benefits |

---

## Edit-Mode Report Format

Bottom line first: one sentence on copy health and biggest fix. Then sweep by sweep. Skip sweep with zero findings, say "clean".

Each finding: **what** wrong → **why** it cost conversion → **fix**, with before/after line. Tag confidence:

- 🟢 clear improvement, apply
- 🟡 judgment call, author choose
- 🔴 need author input (missing proof, unknown terms)

Order findings by conversion impact, not taste. Finish with clean edited copy, then short change log. Author keep final say: offer to re-run after their changes.
